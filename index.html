Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data private and secure?

Yes, your health data is encrypted and stored securely. We adhere to strict privacy policies and never share your personal information without consent.

Can I use DiaBuddy with my glucose monitor?

DiaBuddy works with manual entry from any monitor. We're continually adding direct integration with popular glucose monitoring devices.

Is DiaBuddy free to use?

DiaBuddy offers a free plan with essential features. Premium features are available with a subscription for more advanced tracking and insights.

Can I share my data with my healthcare provider?

Yes, you can generate reports to share with your healthcare team or grant them direct access to your data through our secure sharing features.
